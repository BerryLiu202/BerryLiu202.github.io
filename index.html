About

Xinzhi (Berry) Liu is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the School of Engineering, University of Warwick, working with Prof. Layi Alatise on the reliability and robustness of wide bandgap power semiconductor devices. His current work focuses on reverse-conduction behaviour and short-circuit ruggedness of 650 V GaN monolithic bidirectional switches, combining converter-level characterisation with device-level TCAD.

He received his PhD in Electrical Power Engineering from Universiti Putra Malaysia in 2025, including an 18-month funded research attachment at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), Shenzhen, with Prof. Mengyuan Hua. His thesis on dynamic on-resistance and multi-pulse output capacitance extraction in Schottky p-GaN HEMTs received the IEEE CASS Malaysia Chapter Best PhD Dissertation Award 2025. He also holds an MSc in Green Technology (Energy) with Distinction from Hong Kong Baptist University.

Before his research career he worked on substation protection design and commissioning at Manitoba Hydro, Canada, which shaped how he thinks about protection, failure modes and safety margins.
